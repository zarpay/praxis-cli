# Practices

> **"This is what you own."**

A Practice is a discrete piece of work that gets delegated to an Expert. It answers:
- What needs to be done?
- What does success look like?
- What resources are available?

## Important Distinction

Practices are **owned, not just executed**.

When you delegate a practice, you're saying: "This is yours — figure it out, deliver it, be accountable for it."

### Sections

1. **Objective**: What this practice achieves
2. **Inputs**: What information or resources are needed
3. **Outputs**: What deliverables are expected
4. **Process**: How to accomplish this (high-level)
5. **Criteria**: How success is measured

```md
---
title: "{practice_title}"
type: practice
---
```
