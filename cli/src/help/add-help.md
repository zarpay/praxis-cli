Scaffolds new authoring documents from the templates the compiler
expects, so an author starts from the right shape instead of a blank
page. Templates carry the frontmatter the compiler and the eval layer
honor; the placeholders left in the body are guidance to replace by
hand.

Docs: https://zarpay.github.io/praxis-cli/commands/add
      https://zarpay.github.io/praxis-cli/concepts/knowledge-primitives
