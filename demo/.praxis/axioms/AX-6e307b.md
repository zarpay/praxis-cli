Services must perform all I/O through their injected Store dependency; any external communication or side effects must be mediated by Store methods.

## Violating example

console.log('Sending newsletter to:', recipient);

## Compliant example

// All I/O goes through store methods
await store.logActivity('newsletter_sent', { recipient });
