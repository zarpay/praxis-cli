Services must validate that entities exist and meet eligibility criteria before performing operations on them. Returning computed results without checking whether the subject entity exists or qualifies represents incomplete business logic that could produce meaningless or incorrect outcomes.

## Violating example

A discount service that returns a discount value for a memberId without first checking if the member exists or is eligible for discounts.

## Compliant example

A discount service that queries the Store for the member, returns an error if not found or ineligible, and only computes the discount after confirming eligibility.
